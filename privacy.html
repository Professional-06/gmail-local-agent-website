Privacy Policy

Application: Gmail Local Agent

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Information accessed

With the user's explicit Google authorization, Gmail Local Agent accesses Gmail messages matching the user's selected Promotions search and time period. This may include message subjects, sender details, dates, and message bodies.

2. How information is used

Email information is used to prepare a PDF report requested by the user. The application is designed to process the information on the user's computer.

3. Storage and sharing

The application saves the generated PDF and Google authorization token locally on the user's computer. Users should protect their computer and local files. Email content is not intentionally sold or shared with third parties by the application.

4. Data retention and deletion

Users can delete the generated PDF and revoke the application's Google account access through their Google Account security settings. Users can also remove the locally stored authorization token from the application folder.

5. Security

Users should keep credentials, authorization tokens, and exported PDFs private. No software can guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the application changes. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google consent screen.
